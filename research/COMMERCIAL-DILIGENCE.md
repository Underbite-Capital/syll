## Claims and proof

The project may claim only that current VoiceInk source exposes the required shell and that the local build compiles when verified. It must not claim Wispr Flow parity, acceptable latency, BOYA compatibility or user preference before David's recorded trial.

## Commercial risks and unknowns

The key risk is false momentum: treating a successful build as adoption evidence. Other unknowns are David's actual edit burden, the cost of optional cleanup, and whether permission/paste friction persists across app restarts.

## Recommended next commercial experiment

David uses the build for 20 real prompts and records whether he switched back to Flow. Stop if the local path is clearly worse and no single fixable cause dominates. The decision changed is whether any further engineering is justified.
