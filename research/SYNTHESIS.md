## Smallest credible pilot

- **Named buyer / sponsor:** David.
- **User and painful job:** David dictating jargon-heavy agent prompts quickly and privately.
- **Bounded input and workflow:** BOYA Magic 02, push-to-talk, 20 corpus clips and 20 real prompts in ChatGPT/Claude/Cursor.
- **Deliverable:** Local app plus importable dictionary, prompt, benchmark and trial log.
- **Commercial commitment:** One real work session; no spend or external promise.
- **Technical evidence generated:** Model-specific raw transcripts, release latency, term/general errors, edit burden and failure category.
- **Safety, privacy and authority gates:** Cleanup OFF for local baseline; Auto Send None; David controls macOS permission dialogs and any cloud provider configuration.
- **Review test:** David can say local/Flow and identify the dominant remaining pain.
- **Stop and reversal conditions:** Stop if shell is brittle, latency/quality is clearly worse, dictionary protection fails, or David returns to Flow without an obvious fix.

## What not to build or claim yet

Do not build capture, permissions, shortcut, insertion, model management, onboarding, provider abstractions, Voxtral integration, contextual entity repair or product UI. Do not claim Flow parity, BOYA compatibility, acceptable latency or David preference before the trial.
