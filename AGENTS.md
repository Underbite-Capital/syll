# Project working contract

Read `README.md` and `.underbite/project.json` before acting. Treat `README.md` as the human entry point and `project.json` as routing metadata.

## Research before build

- Complete the relevant sections of `research/COMMERCIAL-DILIGENCE.md` and `research/TECHNICAL-LITERATURE-REVIEW.md` before selecting a product scope or architecture.
- Keep `research/source-register.csv` and `research/claim-evidence.csv` current. Cite primary sources beside consequential claims.
- Label observed fact, source claim, inference, assumption and open question separately.
- Convert research into the smallest decision-changing pilot in `research/SYNTHESIS.md`; do not substitute broad research or synthetic polish for the next real evidence gate.

## Technical integrity

- Preserve supplied artifacts under `reference/` with provenance and digests where material.
- Bind architecture recommendations to source evidence, assumptions, measurable predictions, experiments and reversal conditions.
- Verify implementation, dataset, standard, maintenance and licensing facts at their official source and record the check date.
- Keep customer data, credentials and secret-bearing files out of Git.

## Authority

- Prepare external payloads locally. Send, submit, publish or upload only after David approves the exact destination, final payload and attachments.
- Treat spend, provider creation, production mutation, review acceptance and Done as human gates unless the current task grants exact authority.
- Lead a human handoff with `Action needed from David:` and provide the exact action, return payload and resume condition. Otherwise state `No action needed from David` and continue the agent-owned work.
